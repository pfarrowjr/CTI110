
# How to make a ham sandwich

## Assumptions
- the robot will learn how to make a ham sandwich
- the robot will opn the refrigerator door
- the robot will grab the pieces of bread and open the bag
- they robot may smah the sandwich once it is done

## Instructions
1. grab the bread bag
2. open the bread bag
3. reach in the bread bag and grab 2 pieces of bread
4. set them apart
5. go to the refrigerator and open the door
6. grab the packaged ham out of the refrigerator
7. place it next to the pieces of bread
8. open the ham package 
9. reach in and grab 2 pieces of sliced ham
10. place the 2 pieces of ham and place on one piece of bread
11. pick up the other piece of bread
12. place that piece on top of the bread with the ham

## Done when
- the 2 pieces of bread has the ham between them
